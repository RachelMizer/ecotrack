Pig Info

A healthy adult pig has a normal internal body temperature of 101°F to 103°F (38.3°C to 39.4°C), averaging around 102°F.

Body Temperature Details
Internal Core:  
Adult pigs maintain a core temperature between 101°F and 103°F.
Piglets run warmer, often 102°F to 104°F, due to higher metabolic rates and lower heat retention.

Skin Temperature:  
Typically 6°F to 10°F lower than core temperature because pigs have minimal hair and poor sweat capability.

Danger Zones:

Below 100°F: Hypothermia risk, especially in newborn piglets.

Above 104°F: Early heat stress.

Above 106°F: Severe heat stress; 107°F+ can be fatal without rapid cooling.

Ideal Environmental (Air) Temperatures
Comfort Range:  
Adult pigs thrive between 60°F and 75°F (15°C to 24°C).

Cold Tolerance:  
Pigs tolerate cold moderately well with bedding and shelter, but piglets are extremely vulnerable and require heat lamps or warm creep areas.

Heat Tolerance:  
Pigs are highly prone to heat stress due to poor sweating ability.
Temperatures above 80°F–85°F cause stress; above 90°F require shade, mud wallows, fans, or misters.
High humidity increases risk dramatically.

AVERAGE LIFESPAN
Pigs typically live 10 to 15 years, though lifespan varies significantly based on breed, purpose, and care.

Factors Affecting Lifespan
Breed:  
Hardy breeds like Berkshire, Tamworth, and Large Black often live longer than high‑production commercial hybrids.

Diet and Water:  
Balanced feed, minerals, and constant clean water support long-term health and prevent digestive issues.

Protection:  
Secure fencing, predator control, and shelter from extreme weather reduce injury and early mortality.

Health Care:  
Routine vaccinations, parasite control, hoof care, and monitoring for respiratory diseases extend lifespan.

AGE
Pigs reach adulthood when they achieve sexual maturity, typically between 5 and 6 months, though full physical maturity comes later.

Milestones of Adulthood
Gilts (Females):  
Reach puberty around 5–6 months, depending on breed and nutrition.
Considered adults when they farrow (give birth) for the first time, usually around 12 months.

Boars (Males):  
Reach sexual maturity around 5–7 months, showing mounting and breeding behaviors.
Full physical maturity occurs around 2–3 years.

Full Size:  
While sexually mature early, pigs continue growing until 2–3 years of age, especially larger heritage breeds.
