Chicken Info:

AVERAGE TEMPERATURE
A healthy adult chicken has a normal internal body temperature of 105°F to 107°F (40.6°C to 41.7°C), averaging around 106°F.
Body Temperature Details
• Internal Core: 105°F – 107°F for adults; newly hatched chicks start lower around 103.5°F (39.7°C) before stabilizing by three weeks of age.
• Skin Temperature: Typically about 3.5°F lower than core body temperature.
• Danger Zones: Internal temperatures dropping or rising past normal regulatory limits cause severe stress; core temperatures hitting 113°F to 117°F can be fatal due to heat stroke.
Ideal Environmental (Air) Temperatures
• Comfort Range: Chickens thrive best when ambient temperatures are between 65°F and 75°F (18°C to 24°C).
• Cold Tolerance: Healthy adult chickens tolerate cold weather well down to freezing or even subzero temperatures (-20°F) if they have a dry, draft-free coop and full feathering, though extreme cold risks frostbite on combs and wattles.
• Heat Tolerance: Temperatures consistently above 85°F–90°F trigger heat stress, requiring shade, fresh water, and good ventilation.

AVERAGE LIFESPAN
On average, chickens live between 5 to 10 years, but their lifespan varies a lot depending on their breed, environment, and how they are raised

• Breed: Sturdier heritage breeds like Rhode Island Reds and Orpingtons often live longer than heavy production hybrids.
• Diet and Water: Clean water and proper feed keep their bodies healthy. Older hens often need less calcium and protein.
• Protection: Safe coops that keep out predators and bad weather prevent early deaths.
• Health Care: Regular checks for mites, worms, and diseases help chickens reach old age.

AGE
Chickens are generally considered adults when they reach sexual maturity between 18 and 24 weeks (about 4 to 6 months) of age.
Milestones of Adulthood
• Hens (Pullets): Female chickens reach adulthood at the "point of lay," usually around 18 weeks old, when they start producing their first eggs and can be switched to adult layer feed.
• Roosters (Cockerels): Male chickens reach adulthood when they begin to crow and show mating behaviors, which typically happens around 5 months of age.
• Full Size: While sexually mature at 5 to 6 months, some larger or slower-growing heritage breeds (like Jersey Giants or Brahmas) can take up to 8 months—or nearly 2 years—to reach their absolute maximum physical body size.
