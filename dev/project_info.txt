App Description:

EcoVitals is a next‑generation wildlife telemetry platform designed to give landowners, 
conservation teams, and property managers a clear, real‑time understanding of animal activity 
across their land. By combining precise movement tracking with continuous biometric monitoring, 
EcoVitals delivers a complete picture of animal health, behavior, and habitat use — 
all in one intuitive dashboard.

Development Stack:
Database - PostgreSQL
Storage - Github
Backend - Django/Python
Backend Hosting - Railway
Frontend - React.js
Frontend Hosting - Netlify
