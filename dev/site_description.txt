App / Service name: EcoTrack
Tagline: Where animal health meets land insight

App Description: EcoTrack is a real‑time livestock tracking and health‑monitoring platform built for farms that need clarity, efficiency, 
    and dependable oversight. Designed specifically for cows, pigs, and chickens, EcoTrack maps animal movement across your property, showing live 
    and historical location trails so you can understand grazing patterns, roaming behavior, and activity levels at a glance. Each animal has a 
    dedicated profile where you can record and update vital statistics such as temperature, weight, and other key health indicators, making it easy 
    to monitor wellbeing over time and respond quickly to changes.

    EcoTrack also manages feeding stations, tracking their contents, usage rates, and refill schedules to ensure consistent nutrition and reduce 
    manual checks. By unifying movement data, biometric insights, and feeding‑resource management, EcoTrack gives farmers a clear, actionable 
    view of their livestock operations — supporting healthier animals, more efficient workflows, and smarter land stewardship.

Data:
In /dev/animal data:
    Refer to Chickens.txt, Cows.txt, and Pigs.txt for information on farm-specific animal data.
    Refer to Chickens info.txt, Cows info.txt, and Pigs info.txt for information about the animals that can be used to create stats, charts and graphs.

Pages:
    0) Login Page. This should show the chickens background and a basic login form.
    1) Dashboard: Shows filterable charts and graphs related to the data stored on the site
    2) Catalog: This page should have three buttons "Cows", "Pigs", "Chickens". Each button should lead to an indvidual page for each animal group.
    Each page should have a list of the animals of that type. Each animal should have their individual picture and list of stats. The weight stat and the 
    temperature stat should have a link beside each that links to a table showing that animal's individual weight stats and temperature stats individually.
    The tables on those pages should be filterable. Weights for all animals start at birth/hatching and are taken each week. Temperatures are taken every 
    two days for all animals.
    3) Tracker: A location map of the farm that shows the movement of each animal (as they are tagged with trackers). Clicking a dot should take someone to the 
    animal group page for that animal type--for example, clicking "Amy"'s dot should take a user to the Chickens catalog page and Amy the chicken's entry on that page.
    The map should have subareas that users can click on to view a zoomed in section of the map that shows the coops area, pens area and enclosure areas.
    4) Nutrition: This page should show the amounts of feed in each of the feeders on the farm. Types of feed should be listed for each feeder as well. It should show
    what vaccinations and medications each of the animals are getting.
    5) Schedule: Schedule should show the feeding and medication schedule for each animal. Feed and medicine should change based on what type of animal it is, what its
    age is, and its fertility status--whether an animal is pregnant, breast-feeding / milk-feeding babies.
    6) Account: This page should show the user their account information.
